How to Play

Goal

Move all 52 cards to the four foundation piles in the top right, sorted by suit from Ace to King.

Setup

  • Tableau: 7 columns, with 1 to 7 cards. Only the bottom card of each is face-up.
  • Stock: The remaining 24 face-down cards in the top left.
  • Waste: Cards drawn from the stock land here, face-up.
  • Foundations: 4 empty slots, one per suit (♥ ♦ ♣ ♠).

Moves

  • In the tableau, build downward in alternating colors (red on black, black on red).
  • You can move single cards or groups, as long as the group itself is a valid sequence.
  • Empty tableau columns can only be filled with a King (or a stack starting with a King).
  • Click the stock to flip a new card to the waste. When the stock is empty, click again to recycle it.
  • The top card of the waste can be played to a tableau column or to a foundation.

Tips

  • Double-click a top card to send it to its foundation pile automatically (if it fits).
  • Reveal hidden cards before rushing to fill foundations.
  • Try to keep at least one column empty for shuffling Kings around.

Winning

Each foundation pile must contain all 13 cards of its suit, in order: A, 2, 3, … J, Q, K. Once all four are full, you win.
